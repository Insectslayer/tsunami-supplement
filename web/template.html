Interactive appendix

Tsunami transformations

Uplifting distant regions of a scene so they become more visible to an observer. Every figure below is live: the profile and the uplift slider at the top of the page drive all of them at once, so the same transformation can be followed from a single curve in a vertical plane through to a rendered camera view.

Four uplift profiles — parabolic, hyperbolic, angular, spherical World radius 500 m · observer at 100 m

The text is the appendix as written; the figures replace the static images with live constructions computed in the browser from the same formulas. The profile mathematics is a port of tsunami.py, checked against values produced by that implementation and against tight-tolerance quadrature.

Uplift is parametrised by the transformed boundary angle α′_w rather than by p directly, so that all four profiles are always compared at the same amount of visible spreading; the corresponding p is shown beside the slider.
